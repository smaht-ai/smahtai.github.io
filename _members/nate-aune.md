Nate is an AI engineer, entrepreneur, and open-source advocate based in Greater Boston. He is founder of [Jazkarta](https://jazkarta.com), a Python web development firm serving nonprofits, universities, and startups, and contributes to the [Agentics Foundation](https://agentics.org) on open-source agentic infrastructure.

He previously founded and led [Appsembler](https://appsembler.com), a developer education platform used by companies such as Redis, Chef, and Snowflake, through its acquisition in 2022. Nate organizes the Django Boston meetup, speaks at AI Tinkerers and similar community events, and teaches hands-on agentic engineering for professional developers.
