Andy Singleton has built software, hardware, and financial products. He has exits with SNL Securities (securities research), PowerSteering Software (enterprise SaaS) and Assembla (team SaaS). At Assembla he was a leader of the movement to globally distributed teams and continuous delivery, writing “A Guide to the New Continuous Agile”. He was an early player in tokenized securities with Aboveboard and Maxos. His past research on genetic programming informs his current work on automated improvement of skills, agents, and corporations.
